Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information Uztent (“we”, “us”) collects when you use our live-streaming service, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We process personal data in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan “On Personal Data”.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, email address, first name and last name. We do not receive your Google password. You may also choose a username and, optionally, set a password; we store passwords only as a one-way cryptographic hash.

Sessions. To keep you signed in we issue access and refresh tokens. We store refresh tokens only in hashed form, together with their expiry date.

Streaming information. If you stream, we store your stream settings (title, chat slow mode, stream key) and a record of each broadcast: its title, start and end time, and peak number of viewers. Your video and audio pass through our media server so that viewers can watch them; where recording is enabled, the recording is kept as a video on demand (“VOD”).

Chat. Messages you send in a stream’s chat are stored together with your user ID, your username (or first name, if you have no username), the time of sending, and your role in that chat. Streamers may also store which users they banned or appointed as moderators.

Follows. When you follow another user, we store who you follow and when. Each user’s number of followers is stored with their stream settings.

Technical information. When you watch a stream, our media server processes your IP address and browser user agent to deliver the video and to count concurrent viewers. When you broadcast, the IP address of your streaming software is used to authorize the connection. Viewer counts are stored only as numbers, not linked to individual viewers. Our servers may keep short-lived technical logs for security and troubleshooting.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To run live streams, recordings, chat, and viewer counts.
  • To enforce our Terms of Use, including rate limits, bans and moderation.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. What is public

The following is visible to anyone, including people who are not signed in: your username, your live streams and their titles, viewer counts, your VODs, chat messages together with the sender’s name and role, your number of followers, and the lists of who you follow and who follows you (shown with username, or first name if you have no username). Please do not share personal information in stream titles or chat.

4. Cookies and local storage

Our web app stores a refresh token in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes it. Our media server may set a technical cookie to manage your video session. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

5. Sharing

We share information only as needed to run the service:

  • Google, to verify your sign-in.
  • Our hosting provider in Uzbekistan, which stores and processes data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and others.

6. Retention

  • Account information: until you delete your account.
  • Sessions: until you sign out or the session expires (by default 30 days). Changing your password ends all sessions.
  • VODs and their chat: until the streamer deletes the VOD or the account is deleted. Deleting a VOD deletes its chat.
  • Chat messages: until deleted by the streamer or a moderator, or until the account is deleted.
  • Follows: until you unfollow, or until either account is deleted.
  • Technical logs: up to 30 days.

7. Your choices and rights

You can update your name and username in your profile and delete your own VODs at any time. To access a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and associated data, contact us at khberv.dev@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

We protect your information with measures such as hashed passwords and tokens, encrypted connections where supported, and restricted access to internal systems. No service is completely secure, so please keep your stream key and password private.

9. Children

Uztent has no minimum age. If you are a minor under the laws of your country, you may use Uztent only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. Because streams, chat and usernames are public, we recommend that minors do not share their real name or other personal information. A parent or guardian can ask us to access or delete a minor’s data by writing to khberv.dev@gmail.com.

10. Where your data is stored

Your information is stored and processed on servers in Uzbekistan. If you use Uztent from another country, your information is transferred to Uzbekistan. Google processes your sign-in on its own infrastructure under its own privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: khberv.dev@gmail.com.
